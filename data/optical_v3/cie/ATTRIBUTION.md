# CIE observer dataset

Original data, unchanged: **CIE 2019, Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931
standard colorimetric observer**, International Commission on Illumination (CIE),
Vienna, Austria. DOI: https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b

Primary landing page: https://cie.co.at/datatable/cie-1931-colour-matching-functions-2-degree-observer

Data source: https://files.cie.co.at/Publications-datasets/CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv

Metadata source: https://files.cie.co.at/Publications-datasets/CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv_metadata.json

No observer values were altered. The optical integration routine interpolates
and numerically integrates this table over the explicitly reported 380–780 nm
rendering interval. It normalizes unit radiance response to Y=1 for each specified
synthetic Planck illuminant and reports the omitted photopic source weight.
